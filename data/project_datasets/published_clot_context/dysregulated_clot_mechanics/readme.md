# Dysregulated Clot Mechanics

## Contents

- `VTutwiler_DysregulatedClotMechanics_RawData.xlsx`: Plasma clot stiffness time series, turbidity, thrombin generation, and clinical variables from trauma patients and healthy donors.
- `confocal_images/`: Confocal microscopy images of fibrin networks.
- Supplemental PDF: Methods, figures, and variable descriptions associated with the study.

## Relevance to This Project

Useful for studying clot formation kinetics, final stiffness, fibrin structure, and thrombin generation. The rheology samples are primarily platelet-poor plasma rather than whole blood.

## Source

Gosselin AR et al. *Dysregulated clot mechanics and kinetics impacted by injury severity, predict mortality after trauma*. Shock, 2025.

- Article DOI: https://doi.org/10.1097/SHK.0000000000002544
- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.7282/00000610
