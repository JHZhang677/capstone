# Fortified Whole-Blood Clots

## Contents

- `41467_2025_58204_MOESM10_ESM.xlsx`: Source data for the article figures. The `Figure 2` sheet contains time, storage modulus G', and loss modulus G'' for natural whole-blood clots and whole blood combined with xHA or oHA microparticles. Other sheets contain clotting, adhesion, fibrinolysis, biocompatibility, and animal-hemostasis measurements.

## Rheology Data

The rheology measurements were performed on recalcified citrated sheep whole blood using a HAAKE MARS III rotational rheometer at 37 degrees Celsius. Time sweeps used 10% strain, 1 Hz oscillation, a 0.5 mm gap, and a 300-second duration. The public workbook contains representative numerical G' and G'' time series at approximately 2.5-second intervals, plus replicate gelation-time and final-modulus results.

## Relevance to This Project

The natural whole-blood trace directly records the transition from recalcified liquid blood to a clot and is suitable for comparing STFT/Fourier and wavelet transition detection. The xHA and oHA conditions provide faster clot-forming comparison signals. Limitations are the short 300-second duration, sheep rather than human blood, one representative continuous trace per condition, and the absence of native RheoWin instrument files or raw within-cycle stress, strain, and torque waveforms.
