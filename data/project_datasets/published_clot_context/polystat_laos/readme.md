# PolySTAT LAOS

## Contents

Raw LAOS rheometer data from 36 purified fibrin-clot samples. The files contain time, stress, strain, torque, and related measurements at approximately 0.01%, 0.1%, 1%, 10%, and 100% strain amplitudes.

## Relevance to This Project

Useful for comparing Fourier harmonics and wavelets for detecting strain stiffening, nonlinearity, fibrin-network sliding, yielding, and recovery. The clots were already formed before LAOS testing, so these data cannot identify the onset of clotting from liquid blood. The samples are not whole blood.

## Source

Pichon TJ et al. *Investigating PolySTAT's role in clot contraction and fibrin network mechanics*. Biomaterials Science, 2025.
