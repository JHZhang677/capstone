# Clot Model TEG

## Contents

- `Dataset10.xlsx`: Continuous whole-blood TEG curves, coagulation factors, fitted model parameters, and conventional TEG measurements from 24 trauma patients.
- `Dataset8.xlsx`: Five paired whole-blood TEG, plasma TEG, thrombin-generation, and coagulation-factor datasets.

## Relevance to This Project

This is the most suitable dataset in this collection for detecting clot initiation, growth, maximum strength, and lysis, and for comparing STFT/Fourier and wavelet methods. The signal is TEG amplitude rather than raw stress and strain from a conventional rheometer.

## Source

Ghetmiri DE et al. *Quick model-based viscoelastic clot strength predictions from blood protein concentrations for cybermedical coagulation control*. Nature Communications, 2024.

- Article DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44231-w
