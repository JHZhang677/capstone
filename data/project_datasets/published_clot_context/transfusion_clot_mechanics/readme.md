# Transfusion Clot Mechanics

## Contents

- `VTutwiler_Transfusion_Rheology.xlsx`: Continuous rheology time series sampled every 5 seconds for approximately 60 minutes under hypocoagulable and hypercoagulable trauma-model conditions and blood-product supplementation.
- `VTutwiler_Transfusion_TEGTests.xlsx`: Continuous TEG amplitude curves sampled every 5 seconds for approximately 62 minutes.
- `VTutwiler_Transfusion_Turbidity.xlsx`: Fibrin formation and fibrinolysis time series sampled every 15 seconds for approximately 80 minutes.
- `VTutwiler_Transfusion_Clinical Data.xlsx`: Patient characteristics and derived coagulation, rheology, turbidity, thrombin-generation, and fibrin-structure variables.
- `VTutwiler_Transfusion_Confocal.xlsx`: Quantitative fibrin-network measurements.
- Supplemental PDF: Experimental methods and study definitions.

## Relevance to This Project

This dataset is highly useful for detecting clot initiation, rapid clot growth, stable clot formation, and fibrinolysis, and for comparing STFT/Fourier and wavelet methods. It includes platelet-poor plasma and whole-blood trauma models, multiple coagulation conditions, and repeated measurements. The spreadsheets contain exported numerical time series rather than native proprietary rheometer or TEG instrument files.
